The service

What well pressure tank service involves

Your well pump pushes water into a pressure tank, and the tank holds that water under pressure so it is ready when you open a tap. When the tank or its controls fail, the whole house feels it.

Pressure tank and well system work in your home typically includes:

  • Diagnosing pressure problems from the tank to the taps
  • Checking tank pre-charge and bladder condition
  • Replacing failed or waterlogged pressure tanks
  • Pressure switch and gauge replacement
  • Tank tee, valves and fitting repairs
  • Setting cut-in and cut-out pressure correctly
  • Leak-checking every connection
  • Testing pressure and flow at your fixtures

Common problems

Signs your pressure tank or well system needs attention

These are the symptoms that usually point to the pressure tank or the equipment around it:

Rapid on-off cycling

If the pump clicks on and off every few seconds while water runs, the tank may be waterlogged. That wears out pumps fast.

Pressure that surges and drops

Showers that go strong-weak-strong often mean the tank has lost its air charge.

Low pressure throughout the house

Weak flow everywhere can come from switch settings, a clogged filter or tank problems.

Water at the tank

Rust, sweating or leaks around the tank or tank tee mean it is time for repair or replacement.

A tank that sounds full of water

Tapping the tank should sound hollow near the top. A solid sound everywhere suggests a failed bladder.

No water at all

Could be the switch, wiring or the pump itself. We will track down the cause.

Pump in the well? If the problem turns out to be the pump down in the well itself, we’ll tell you so you can bring in the right well specialist. The pressure tank and the equipment in your home are what we service.

Project considerations

What to consider for your well system

A few details make the difference between a quick fix and a lasting one.

Tank size
A larger tank means fewer pump starts, which helps your pump last longer. The right size depends on your pump and household.
Pump protection
Short cycling from a failed tank is one of the main reasons well pumps wear out early. Fixing the tank protects the pump.
Pressure settings
Switch settings and tank pre-charge have to match. Many pressure complaints come down to settings being out of sync.
Water quality
Sediment, iron and hard water clog filters and wear out tanks and fixtures. Treatment may be part of the solution.
Location & access
Tanks in tight basements or crawl spaces can affect the work. We plan for access during the visit.
Power & safety
Well equipment involves both water and electricity. Repairs should be done with power safely shut off.
