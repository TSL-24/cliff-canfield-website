The service

What our plumbing repair service covers

Most plumbing problems start small: a drip, a slow leak, a valve that won’t close. Fixing them early is almost always cheaper than waiting for water damage.

Every plumbing repair visit follows the same approach:

  • Listening to what you have noticed and when
  • Finding the actual source, not just the symptom
  • Explaining the problem and your options
  • A clear price before any work begins
  • Quality parts that match your system
  • Repairs done neatly with minimal disruption
  • Testing the repair and checking for leaks
  • Cleaning up before we leave

Common problems

Common plumbing problems we repair

These are the repairs homeowners call us for most often:

Leaks and drips

Leaking pipes, fittings, supply lines and valves, including leaks in hidden spots that can cause damage.

Faucet problems

Drips, low flow, loose handles and leaks under the sink. Some faucets need a repair, others a replacement.

Running toilets

A toilet that runs or refills on its own can waste a lot of water. Usually a quick fix.

Low water pressure

Weak pressure at one fixture or the whole house, from clogged aerators to failing valves or piping.

Stuck or leaking valves

Old shut-off valves that won’t turn or that leak when they do. Important to fix before an emergency.

Noisy pipes

Banging, hammering or whistling pipes are often fixable and can signal pressure problems.

Water where it shouldn’t be? Find your main shut-off valve and turn it off, then call us. Stopping the water first limits damage while we’re on the way.

Repair or replace

Plumbing repair options

Not every problem needs a replacement. We’ll walk you through the options honestly:

Most common

Targeted repair

Fix the specific part that failed, such as a cartridge, washer, fitting or valve.

  • Lowest cost
  • Quick turnaround
  • Keeps what still works
Longer lasting

Component replacement

Replace a worn faucet, valve or section of pipe when repairs would just buy time.

  • New warranty on parts
  • Fewer future calls
  • Better performance
Prevention

Shut-off & valve upgrades

Replace old, stuck shut-off valves so you can actually turn the water off when it matters.

  • Quick to install
  • Peace of mind
  • Easier future repairs
Bigger picture

System updates

When problems keep coming back, updating sections of old piping can be the most cost-effective fix.

  • Fewer leaks
  • Better pressure
  • Planned, not emergency

What helps us fix your plumbing faster

A few details help us diagnose the problem and bring the right parts.

When it started
Tell us when you first noticed the problem and whether it is constant or only happens sometimes.
Where it is
Note which fixture, room or area is affected, and whether more than one spot has the problem.
Photos
A quick photo of the leak, fixture or model label can help us plan the repair.
Your water source
Let us know if you are on a private well or city water. It affects pressure and fixtures.
Shut-off location
Know where your main shut-off is. We can label it for you if you’re not sure.
Access
Clearing space under sinks or around the problem area helps us get started right away.
