The service

What toilet repair and replacement involves

Toilet problems are some of the most common calls we get, and some of the most wasteful if ignored. A running toilet can waste a surprising amount of water every day.

Toilet repair or replacement typically includes:

  • Diagnosing running, leaking or flushing problems
  • Replacing flappers, fill valves and flush parts
  • Fixing leaks at the base, tank or supply line
  • Resetting loose toilets on a new wax ring or foam seal
  • Checking the flange and floor for damage
  • Removing the old toilet
  • Installing and leveling the new toilet
  • New supply line and shut-off valve if needed

Common problems

Signs your toilet needs repair or replacement

These are the toilet problems we see most often:

Constantly running

Water that keeps running or refills on its own usually means a worn flapper or fill valve.

Water at the base

Leaks around the base often mean a failed seal. Left alone, they damage the floor below.

Wobbly toilet

A toilet that rocks can break its seal and leak. It usually needs to be reset.

Weak or double flushing

Poor flushing can come from worn parts, low water level or an old, inefficient design.

Cracks in the tank or bowl

Cracked porcelain can’t be reliably repaired and should be replaced.

Old, water-hungry toilet

Older toilets use far more water per flush than modern efficient models.

Project considerations

What to consider before replacing a toilet

A few details make sure your new toilet fits and works well.

Rough-in size
Measure from the wall to the floor bolts. Most toilets need a 12-inch rough-in, but older homes may differ.
Height
Comfort-height toilets are a few inches taller and easier for many people to use.
Bowl shape
Elongated bowls are more comfortable; round bowls fit smaller bathrooms better.
Water use
Efficient toilets save water every flush, which matters on both city water and private wells.
Floor condition
A long-leaking toilet may have damaged the flange or subfloor. We check before installing.
Your own toilet
Already bought a toilet? We can install it. Let us know the model so we can check the fit.
