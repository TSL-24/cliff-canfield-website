About us

Local plumbing, done right the first time.

Cliff Canfield Plumbing is a locally owned, owner-operated plumbing company serving New Milford and the surrounding Western Connecticut area. When you call, you work directly with Cliff, a licensed master plumber who takes pride in quality work and attention to detail.

We handle everything from fixture repairs and water heater replacements to complete bathroom remodels, water treatment, gas systems, and well equipment, for homeowners as well as general contractors and home builders.

Every project starts the same way: we come out, talk through what you need, and give you a clear quote before any work begins.
